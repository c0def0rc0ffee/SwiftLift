A small noticeboard for the Bailiwick. If you make the same drive most weeks, like the school run, the commute, or a weekly trip to St Sampson, drop two pins on a map and meet other islanders going the same way.

  • Free, community-run, Guernsey only
  • Match by route, days and time
  • Filter by age and sex if you want
  • Chat in-app once a lift is accepted
